Favourite places

My top most favourite places that I happened to visit are: Mt.Kilimanjaro and Hells Gate National Park. I have been to both of these places and they are really beautiful. I would love to visit them again in the future.

Mt.Kilimanjaro

Mt.Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa and is a popular destination for climbers and tourists alike.

Standing at 5,895 meters (19,341 feet) above sea level, your journey to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro—the "Roof of Africa"—is one of the most remarkable trekking experiences in the world. Traversing Africa's highest peak takes you through five distinct climate zones in just a matter of days.

Hells Gate National Park

Hells Gate National Park is a beautiful national park known for its dramatic landscapes and diverse wildlife.

Hells Gate National Park in Naivasha offers one of Kenya's most interactive and adventure-filled outdoor experiences. Unlike most national parks where visitors are restricted to safari vehicles, this unique landscape permits active, self-guided exploration among free-roaming wildlife.
